About

My Story

I graduated from UBC in mechanical engineering in 2024 and joined Critical Environment Technologies as an application engineer. For a year I was the person customers called: 1,700+ system quotations, 200+ technical calls, 100+ system layouts. I learned exactly where the products hurt, because I was the one apologizing for it.

Eleven months in, I moved into product. Today I own product across five product lines. I built the company's first intake and prioritization system, introduced its first sprint cadence, led a zero-to-one hardware program from discovery through certification, and shipped the software that replaced the tools I used to complain about.

The through line is that I do not hand off. I run the discovery, build the business case, write the spec, sit in the sprint, and then check the result against the thing a technician actually installs.
